## D. Protocols & thresholds
*Standard content follows national safety bundles (AIM / CMQCC, ACOG bulletins) — very likely aligned
with our cases; the exact order sets, doses, and local thresholds are institution-specific.*

**Q11 — Anti-D protocol?** 🟢 Predicted: 300 mcg at ~28 weeks and within 72 h postpartum if infant
Rh-positive; also after any sensitizing event. *(High on content; medium on local FMH-testing steps.)*

**Q12 — Severe-range BP + magnesium?** 🟢 Predicted: first-line for severe-range BP =
**labetalol, hydralazine, or immediate-release nifedipine**; **magnesium sulfate for seizure
prophylaxis** (loading + maintenance per bundle); calcium gluconate as antidote. Matches our cards.
*(High on content.)*

**Q13 — PPH bundle?** 🟢 Predicted: stage-based AIM/CMQCC hemorrhage bundle; quantified blood loss,
uterotonic ladder, massive-transfusion activation with ongoing loss. *(High on content; the exact
local trigger volume: low.)*

**Q14 — PPROM regimen?** 🟡 Predicted: latency antibiotics (ampicillin + erythromycin course),
corticosteroids, expectant management to ~34 weeks absent infection. *(Medium — cutoffs vary.)*

**Q15 — GDM thresholds + neonatal glucose?** 🟡 Predicted: two-step screen (50 g → 100 g OGTT); the
**2024 ACOG update refined thresholds but retained the two-step option**, which an updated program
teaches; neonatal glucose screening for infants of diabetic mothers with early feeding. *(Medium.)*

**Q16 — Arrest / induction-failure / TOLAC definitions?** 🟢 Predicted: active-phase **labor arrest**
= ≥ 6 cm, ruptured membranes, no cervical change ≥ 4 h with adequate contractions (or ≥ 6 h if
inadequate); failed induction per ACOG-SMFM OCC No. 1. Note the current term is "labor arrest," not
the outdated "failure to progress." *(High on content.)*

**Q17 — Emergency team / triage destination?** 🔴 Predicted default: warning-sign calls routed to
**OB triage** rather than the ED. Purely institutional — confirm. *(Low.)*

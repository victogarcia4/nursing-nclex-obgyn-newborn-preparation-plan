## 1. Contraception method comparison

| Method | Estrogen? | Key contraindication | Teaching pearl |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combined pill (COC) | Yes | Migraine w/ aura, VTE, smoker ≥ 35 | Daily adherence; ACHES danger signs [CDC US MEC] |
| Progestin-only pill | No | Few; strict timing | Same-time daily dosing [CDC US MEC] |
| LNG-IUD | No | Current pelvic infection | Lightens menses; PATCH danger signs [CDC US MEC] |
| Copper IUD | No | Current pelvic infection | Non-hormonal; also EC up to 5 days [CDC US MEC] |
| Implant | No | Few | Most effective; no daily action [CDC US MEC] |
| DMPA injection | No | — | Every 13 weeks; reversible bone-density effect [CDC US MEC] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) migraine **with aura** contraindicates estrogen; (2) the **copper IUD** is the most effective emergency contraception; (3) LARC = highest effectiveness with no daily adherence.

## 2. Screening vs diagnostic timeline (gestational-age axis)

| Test | Window | Screening or diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| cfDNA / NIPT | ≥ 10 weeks | Screening [ACOG PB 226] |
| Nuchal translucency + serum | 10 3/7–13 6/7 wk | Screening [ACOG PB 226] |
| CVS | 10–13 weeks | Diagnostic [ACOG PB 226] |
| Quad screen | 15–22 weeks | Screening [ACOG PB 226] |
| Amniocentesis | ≥ 15 weeks | Diagnostic [ACOG PB 226] |
| 1-hr GCT → 3-hr OGTT | 24–28 weeks | Screen → diagnostic [ACOG PB 190] |
| GBS culture | 36 0/7–37 6/7 wk | Screening [ACOG CO 797] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) a positive **screen** estimates risk and needs a **diagnostic** confirm; (2) GBS is cultured at **36–37 weeks**; (3) CVS is earlier than amniocentesis.

## 5. GTPAL worked examples

| History | G | T | P | A | L |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Currently pregnant, one 39-wk birth (living) | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| Pregnant; twins at 35 wk (both live); 1 miscarriage | 3 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Pregnant; term son; twins 35 wk (live); 1 abortion | 4 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 |

**Rules:** G = all pregnancies incl. current · T ≥ 37 wk · P 20–36 6/7 wk · A < 20 wk · L living children. **Multiples count once for parity, individually for Living.** [Lowdermilk]

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) count the **current** pregnancy in Gravida; (2) a 35-week birth is **Preterm**, not Term; (3) **twins = one** birth event for T/P but two for L.

## 6a. Lab values — hematology & metabolic (pregnancy vs non-pregnant)

| Lab | Non-pregnant | Pregnancy change |
|---|---|---|
| Hematocrit | 36–46% | ↓ (dilutional/physiologic anemia) [Lowdermilk] |
| WBC | 4–10 k | ↑ (up to ~15 k normal) [Lowdermilk] |
| Fibrinogen | 200–400 | ↑ (hypercoagulable) [Lowdermilk] |
| Fasting glucose | — | Screen 24–28 wk for GDM [ACOG PB 190] |

## 6b. Lab values — dating & Rh

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Naegele's rule | LMP − 3 months + 7 days (+ 1 yr) [Lowdermilk] |
| Ultrasound dating | Supersedes Naegele when cycles irregular [Lowdermilk] |
| Rh(D) immune globulin | 28 wk + within 72 h postpartum if infant Rh+ [ACOG PB 181] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) hematocrit **falls** (dilutional); (2) pregnancy is **hypercoagulable** (fibrinogen up); (3) anti-D at **28 weeks and within 72 h** postpartum for an Rh+ infant.

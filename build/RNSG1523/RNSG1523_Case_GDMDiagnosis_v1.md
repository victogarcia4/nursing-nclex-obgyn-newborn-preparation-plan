# Unfolding Case (WO-G) — GDM Diagnosis and Teaching
**Course:** RNSG 1523 Nursing I · **Traces to objectives:** 1523-D3-2, 1523-D3-3, 1523-D4-1 · **Format:** 6 scenes, one per NCJMM operation.

**Patient:** Rosa, 31, G3 P2, at 26 weeks. BMI 31 prepregnancy; prior infant weighed 4,200 g at birth.

---

### Scene 1 — Recognize Cues (highlight)
**Chart:** 1-hour 50 g glucose challenge test today = **156 mg/dL** (non-fasting). Reports increased thirst. Prior macrosomic infant. BMI 31.
**Item.** Highlight the findings that raise concern for gestational diabetes.
**Key:** elevated 1-hour screen (156), prior macrosomia, and elevated BMI as risk factors.
**Rationale.** An abnormal screen plus risk factors flags GDM risk [ACOG PB 190].

### Scene 2 — Analyze Cues (matrix)
**Item.** Classify each test in the GDM pathway as *Screening* or *Diagnostic*.

| Test | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1-hour 50 g glucose challenge | Screening |
| 3-hour 100 g oral glucose tolerance test | Diagnostic |
| HbA1c (adjunct) | Supportive, not the diagnostic OGTT |

**Rationale.** An abnormal 1-hour screen is confirmed by the diagnostic 3-hour OGTT [ACOG PB 190; ACOG 2024].

### Scene 3 — Prioritize Hypotheses (cloze/dropdown)
**Item.** After the abnormal screen, the priority next step is **[the 3-hour oral glucose tolerance test]**, because the screen **[estimates risk but does not diagnose]**.
**Rationale.** Do not begin treatment on a screen alone; confirm first [ACOG PB 190].

### Scene 4 — Generate Solutions (extended multiple response)
**Scenario:** The 3-hour OGTT confirms GDM (two abnormal values). Rosa is class A1 (diet-controlled candidate).
**Item.** Select the 3 first-line management/teaching elements.
- ✅ Medical nutrition therapy with distributed carbohydrates
- ✅ Regular physical activity as advised
- ✅ Blood-glucose self-monitoring with target teaching
- ❌ Start insulin immediately for all diet-controlled patients
- ❌ Eliminate all carbohydrates
- ❌ Recommend fasting days

**Rationale.** GDM begins with nutrition, activity, and monitoring; insulin is added only if targets are missed (class A2) [ACOG PB 190].

### Scene 5 — Take Actions (drag-and-drop ordering)
**Item.** Order the teaching-visit actions.
1. Confirm understanding of the diagnosis and self-monitoring schedule
2. Demonstrate glucometer use and record-keeping
3. Provide an individualized nutrition plan and activity guidance
4. Establish glucose targets and the plan for when they are exceeded

**Rationale.** Understanding and monitoring skills precede targets and escalation planning [ACOG PB 190].

### Scene 6 — Evaluate Outcomes (trend)
**Two-week log:** Week 1 fasting values 88–96 mg/dL, some 1-hour postprandials 135–142; Week 2 after nutrition adjustments, fasting 82–90 and postprandials mostly < 130.
**Item.** Which interpretation best evaluates the response?
**Key:** Glucose values are trending into target with nutrition therapy — diet control is working; continue current plan and monitoring.
**Rationale.** Evaluate GDM management by the glucose *trend* toward targets, escalating to insulin only if the trend fails [ACOG PB 190].

---

## Debrief — three decision points students most often miss
1. **Treating on the screen.** The 1-hour test is a screen; diagnosis requires the 3-hour OGTT.
2. **Jumping to insulin.** First-line is nutrition, activity, and monitoring; insulin is reserved for missed targets.
3. **Over-restricting carbohydrates.** Balanced, distributed carbohydrate intake — not elimination or fasting — is the goal.

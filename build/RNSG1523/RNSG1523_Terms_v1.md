## OB history & dating

| Front | Back | Diff |
|---|---|---|
| GTPAL | Gravida, Term, Preterm, Abortions, Living — five-element pregnancy-history shorthand. | 2 |
| Gravida (G) | Total number of pregnancies including the current one, regardless of outcome. | 1 |
| Para (P) | Number of births after 20 weeks, whether live or stillborn. | 2 |
| Term (GTPAL T) | Births at or beyond 37 0/7 weeks of gestation. | 1 |
| Preterm (GTPAL P) | Births from 20 0/7 through 36 6/7 weeks of gestation. | 1 |
| Abortions (GTPAL A) | Pregnancy losses before 20 weeks, spontaneous or induced. | 1 |
| Living (GTPAL L) | Number of children currently alive, regardless of current age. | 1 |
| LMP | Last Menstrual Period; the first day of it anchors Naegele's rule. | 1 |
| EDB | Estimated Date of Birth; modern preferred term for the due date. | 1 |
| EDC | Estimated Date of Confinement; traditional term for the due date. | 1 |
| GA | Gestational Age, expressed in completed weeks plus days (e.g., 36 0/7). | 1 |
| AMA | Advanced Maternal Age; 35 years or older at delivery. | 1 |
| Naegele's rule | LMP minus 3 months, plus 7 days, plus 1 year; assumes a 28-day cycle. | 2 |
| EDD | Estimated Date of Delivery; synonym for the due date, used interchangeably with EDB. | 1 |
| 0/7 notation | Weeks plus completed days; '36 0/7' means exactly 36 weeks and 0 days. | 1 |

## Discrimination

| Front | Back | Diff |
|---|---|---|
| Distinguish presumptive from probable signs | Presumptive = subjective (amenorrhea, nausea); probable = examiner-observed (Goodell, positive test). | 3 |
| Distinguish probable from positive signs | Probable can have other causes; positive proves a fetus: FHR, fetal movement felt by examiner, ultrasound. | 3 |
| Distinguish screening from diagnostic tests | Screening estimates risk (cfDNA, quad); diagnostic confirms with karyotype (CVS, amniocentesis). | 3 |
| Distinguish GCT from OGTT | GCT is the 1-hour non-fasting screen; OGTT is the 3-hour fasting diagnostic. | 2 |
| Distinguish EDC/EDB from GA | EDB is the due date; GA is how far along now, in weeks and days. | 2 |
| Distinguish COC from POP eligibility | Estrogen (COC) is contraindicated with high VTE or migraine-with-aura risk; POP is safer there. | 3 |
| Distinguish primary from secondary dysmenorrhea | Primary has no pelvic pathology; secondary is caused by identifiable disease. | 2 |
| Distinguish PLLR from letter categories | PLLR is narrative risk labeling; A/B/C/D/X are the removed legacy letters. | 2 |
| Distinguish CVS from amniocentesis timing | CVS at 10–13 weeks gives an earlier karyotype; amniocentesis is done at 15+ weeks. | 2 |
| Distinguish presumptive discomfort from warning sign | Nausea and urinary frequency are normal; vaginal bleeding and severe headache are warning signs. | 3 |
| Distinguish LARC from short-acting methods | LARC (IUD, implant) needs no daily adherence; pills and rings depend on consistent use. | 2 |
| Distinguish reactive from non-reactive NST | Reactive shows adequate accelerations and is reassuring; non-reactive needs further testing. | 2 |
| Distinguish VMS from GSM | Vasomotor symptoms are hot flashes/night sweats; GSM is vaginal and urinary tissue change. | 2 |

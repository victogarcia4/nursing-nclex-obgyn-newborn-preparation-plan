## 1. Previa vs abruption (Hemorrhage)

| Feature | Placenta previa | Abruptio placentae |
|---|---|---|
| Bleeding | Painless, bright red | Painful, dark |
| Uterus | Soft, non-tender | Rigid, tender, high tone |
| Bleeding may be concealed | No | Yes |
| Digital exam | Contraindicated until previa excluded | Avoid; assess/monitor |
| Key risks | Hemorrhage | DIC, fetal compromise |

*Source: [Lowdermilk].* **The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) **no digital exam** with undiagnosed late bleeding; (2) pain + tone = abruption; (3) abruption → **DIC** risk.

---

## 2. The 4 Ts of postpartum hemorrhage (Hemorrhage)

| T | Cause | First-line response |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Uterine atony (most common) | Massage + oxytocin, empty bladder [ACOG PB 183] |
| Trauma | Laceration/hematoma | Inspect and repair [ACOG PB 183] |
| Tissue | Retained placenta | Removal/evacuation [ACOG PB 183] |
| Thrombin | Coagulopathy/DIC | Correct clotting factors [ACOG PB 183] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) **Tone** causes most PPH — treat first; (2) a boggy displaced fundus = atony + full bladder; (3) QBL beats visual estimation.

## 3. Uterotonic drug card (Hemorrhage)

| Drug | Class | Avoid in |
|---|---|---|
| Oxytocin | First-line | (rare) [ACOG PB 183] |
| Methylergonovine | Ergot | **Hypertension** [ACOG PB 183] |
| Carboprost (PGF2α) | Prostaglandin | **Asthma** [ACOG PB 183] |
| Misoprostol | Prostaglandin | Often usable when others contraindicated [ACOG PB 183] |
| Tranexamic acid | Antifibrinolytic | Give within 3 h as adjunct [ACOG PB 183] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) methylergonovine ✗ in **hypertension**; (2) carboprost ✗ in **asthma**; (3) TXA within **3 hours**.

## 4. Magnesium sulfate card (Hypertension)

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | **Seizure prophylaxis** — NOT an antihypertensive [ACOG PB 222] |
| Also used | Neuroprotection < 32 wk in preterm birth [ACOG PB 234] |
| Toxicity sequence | Loss of DTRs → respiratory depression → cardiac arrest [ACOG PB 222] |
| Monitor | DTRs, respirations, urine output, level [ACOG PB 222] |
| Antidote | **Calcium gluconate** at bedside [ACOG PB 222] |
| First toxicity action | **Stop infusion**, give calcium gluconate [ACOG PB 222] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) magnesium ≠ antihypertensive; (2) **reflexes disappear first** in toxicity; (3) antidote = **calcium gluconate**.

## 5. FHR category → action algorithm (Hypoxia)

| Category | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| I | Normal | Routine monitoring [ACOG 2025] |
| II | Indeterminate | Intrauterine resuscitation, evaluate [ACOG 2025] |
| III | Abnormal | Expedite delivery [ACOG 2025] |

**Intrauterine resuscitation** = reposition, IV fluids, reduce uterotonics, treat hypotension. **Routine maternal oxygen is NOT recommended for Category II/III when maternal SpO₂ is adequate** (2025 reversal, supersedes PB 106/116) [ACOG 2025]. `[VERIFY: 2025 FHR CPG document number — acog.org]`

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) Category II → **resuscitate**, not immediate delivery; (2) Category III → **expedite birth**; (3) **no routine O₂** without maternal hypoxia.

## 6. VEAL CHOP (Hypoxia)

| See (VEAL) | Think (CHOP) | Concern? |
|---|---|---|
| **V**ariable decel | **C**ord compression | Reposition |
| **E**arly decel | **H**ead compression | Benign |
| **A**ccelerations | **O**K | Reassuring |
| **L**ate decel | **P**lacental insufficiency | Concerning — act |

*Source: [Macones et al. 2008].* **The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) **Late = placental insufficiency** (concerning); (2) Early = head compression (benign); (3) Variable = cord compression (reposition).

| Tocolytic | Class | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Nifedipine | Calcium channel blocker | Common first-line [ACOG PB 171] |
| Indomethacin | NSAID | < 32 wk, short course [ACOG PB 171] |
| Magnesium sulfate | — | Neuroprotection role < 32 wk [ACOG PB 234] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) tocolytics buy time for **steroids**; (2) indomethacin limited to **< 32 wk**; (3) **17-OHPC withdrawn** — no longer standard.

## 8. Emergency action sequences (first moves)

| Emergency | First move |
|---|---|
| Cord prolapse | Lift presenting part off cord; knee-chest/Trendelenburg; keep hand in place [Lowdermilk] |
| Shoulder dystocia | Call help; **McRoberts** + suprapubic pressure (HELPERR) [ACOG-SMFM OCC 1] |
| Uterine rupture (TOLAC) | Stop oxytocin; prepare **emergency cesarean** [ACOG PB 205] |
| PPH (atony) | Fundal massage + oxytocin; empty bladder; escalate [ACOG PB 183] |
| Tachysystole + Cat II | **Stop/decrease oxytocin**, reposition, fluids [ACOG-SMFM OCC 1] |

**The three things most likely to be tested:** (1) cord prolapse → **relieve compression first, hand stays**; (2) shoulder dystocia → **McRoberts first**; (3) tachysystole → **reduce oxytocin**, do not increase.

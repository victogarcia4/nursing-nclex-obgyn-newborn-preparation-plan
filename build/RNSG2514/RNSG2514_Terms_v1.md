## Discrimination

| Front | Back | Diff |
|---|---|---|
| Distinguish previa from abruption | Previa: painless bright-red bleeding, soft uterus. Abruption: painful dark bleeding, rigid tender uterus. | 3 |
| Distinguish PROM from PPROM | PROM is rupture at ≥37 weeks; PPROM is rupture before 37 weeks with higher infection risk. | 3 |
| Distinguish symmetric from asymmetric FGR | Symmetric FGR is early and global; asymmetric spares the head, suggesting placental insufficiency. | 3 |
| Distinguish Category I/II/III | I normal, no action; II indeterminate, resuscitate and evaluate; III abnormal, expedite delivery. | 3 |
| Distinguish gHTN from preeclampsia | gHTN is hypertension alone; preeclampsia adds proteinuria or end-organ dysfunction. | 3 |
| Distinguish gHTN from chronic HTN | gHTN starts after 20 weeks; chronic hypertension predates pregnancy or precedes 20 weeks. | 2 |
| Distinguish HELLP from preeclampsia | HELLP adds hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, and low platelets; may occur with mild BP. | 3 |
| Distinguish vasa previa from previa | Vasa previa rupture bleeds the fetus; placenta previa bleeding is maternal. | 3 |
| Distinguish early from late decelerations | Early mirror contractions (head compression, benign); late lag behind (placental insufficiency, concerning). | 3 |
| Distinguish variable from late decelerations | Variable are abrupt and vary (cord compression); late are gradual and delayed (placental insufficiency). | 3 |
| Distinguish MgSO₄ role from antihypertensive | Magnesium prevents seizures; labetalol/hydralazine/nifedipine lower blood pressure. | 3 |
| Distinguish tocolytic from uterotonic | Tocolytics (nifedipine) stop contractions; uterotonics (oxytocin) cause them to treat hemorrhage. | 3 |

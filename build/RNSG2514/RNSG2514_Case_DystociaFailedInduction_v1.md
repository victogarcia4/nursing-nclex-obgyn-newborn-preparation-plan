# Unfolding Case (WO-G) — Dystocia and Failed Induction
**Course:** RNSG 2514 Nursing III · **Traces to objectives:** 2514-D3-4, 2514-D3-5 · **Pathway:** Labor dysfunction · **Format:** 6 scenes, one per NCJMM operation.

**Patient:** Bianca, 28, G1 P0, at 41 weeks, admitted for induction of labor. Bishop score 3 on admission.

---

### Scene 1 — Recognize Cues (highlight)
**Chart:** Bishop score 3 (unfavorable cervix). After cervical ripening and oxytocin, the monitor shows 7 contractions in 10 minutes averaged over 30 minutes; the FHR shows recurrent late decelerations with minimal variability.
**Item.** Highlight the cues of tachysystole with a concerning tracing.
**Key:** > 5 contractions/10 min over 30 min (tachysystole) + recurrent late decelerations + minimal variability.
**Rationale.** Excess uterine activity with a Category II tracing signals over-stimulation compromising the fetus [ACOG-SMFM OCC No. 1].

### Scene 2 — Analyze Cues (matrix)
**Item.** For each finding, mark *Tachysystole/Category II concern* or *Normal labor*.

| Finding | Answer |
|---|---|
| 7 contractions/10 min over 30 min | Tachysystole concern |
| Recurrent late decelerations | Category II concern |
| Minimal variability | Category II concern |
| 4 contractions/10 min with moderate variability | Normal labor |

**Rationale.** Tachysystole plus late decelerations and reduced variability define the concern to act on [ACOG-SMFM OCC No. 1].

### Scene 3 — Prioritize Hypotheses (cloze/dropdown)
**Item.** The immediate problem is **[oxytocin-induced tachysystole with a Category II tracing]**, and the priority is to **[reduce uterine stimulation and perform intrauterine resuscitation]**.
**Rationale.** Remove the correctable cause (excess stimulation) before escalating [ACOG-SMFM OCC No. 1].

### Scene 4 — Generate Solutions (extended multiple response)
**Item.** Select the 3 correct interventions.
- ✅ Stop or decrease the oxytocin
- ✅ Reposition the patient (e.g., left lateral) and give an IV fluid bolus
- ✅ Reassess the tracing and notify the provider
- ❌ Apply routine maternal oxygen despite a normal maternal SpO₂
- ❌ Increase the oxytocin to "push through"
- ❌ Take no action while variability is minimal

**Rationale.** Reduce stimulation and resuscitate; routine oxygen is not indicated when maternal saturation is normal [ACOG 2025 intrapartum FHR CPG].

### Scene 5 — Take Actions (drag-and-drop ordering)
**Item.** Order the nurse's actions.
1. Decrease or stop the oxytocin
2. Reposition and give an IV fluid bolus
3. Reassess contractions and the FHR tracing
4. Notify the provider and prepare for possible cesarean if no improvement
5. Document the event and interventions

**Rationale.** Correct the stimulation, resuscitate, reassess, then escalate if needed [ACOG-SMFM OCC No. 1].

### Scene 6 — Evaluate Outcomes (trend)
**Trend after intervention:** hour 0 — 7 contractions/10 min, late decels, minimal variability; hour 1 — 5 contractions/10 min, occasional late decel, variability returning; hour 2 — 4 contractions/10 min, no decels, moderate variability. Cervix remains 4 cm with no further change over 4 hours despite adequate contractions.
**Item.** Which interpretation should drive the next decision?
**Key:** Intrauterine resuscitation resolved the tracing, but the labor now meets criteria for arrest (no cervical change with adequate contractions), so cesarean birth is anticipated.
**Rationale.** Evaluate on two fronts: the tracing improved (resuscitation worked), while arrest of dilation — not "failure to progress" — now guides delivery planning [ACOG-SMFM OCC No. 1].

---

## Debrief — three decision points students most often miss
1. **Increasing oxytocin during tachysystole.** The fix is less stimulation, not more.
2. **Reaching for routine oxygen.** With a normal maternal SpO₂, routine oxygen is no longer recommended for Category II tracings.
3. **Saying "failure to progress."** Document *labor arrest* with the defined stage and criteria.

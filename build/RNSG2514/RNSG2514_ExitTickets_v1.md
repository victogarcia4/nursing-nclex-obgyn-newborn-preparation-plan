**Exit ticket (5):**

| # | Type | Item | Answer | Remediation pointer |

## DAY 3 — Preterm Labor, PROM/PPROM, Induction, Dysfunctional Labor

**Exit ticket (5):**

| # | Type | Item | Answer | Remediation pointer |

## DAY 4 — Operative Birth, FGR, Rupture, Cord Emergencies, Neonate, Demise

**Prior-knowledge poll (3):**
1. What is the first action in cord prolapse?
2. Per current guidance, why is routine maternal oxygen no longer recommended for Category II/III when maternal saturation is normal?
3. What is the priority NRP step for a non-breathing newborn?

**Exit ticket (5):**

| # | Type | Item | Answer | Remediation pointer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recall | First action in cord prolapse? | Lift presenting part off the cord (hand stays) | 2514-D4-4 · HighYield §8 · [Lowdermilk] |
| 2 | Recall | VEAL CHOP: Late decelerations → cause? | Placental insufficiency | 2514-D4-5 · HighYield §6 · [Macones 2008] |
| 3 | Recall | Priority NRP step for a non-breathing newborn? | Positive pressure ventilation (PPV) | 2514-D4-6 · [Weiner & Zaichkin 2021] |
| 4 | Application | Category II, recurrent late decels, maternal SpO₂ 99% — O₂ by mask? | No — routine O₂ not recommended; do intrauterine resuscitation | 2514-D4-5 · HighYield §5 · [ACOG 2025] |
| 5 | Application | TOLAC: sudden tearing pain, loss of station, bradycardia — concern + action? | Uterine rupture → stop oxytocin, emergency cesarean | 2514-D4-3 · HighYield §8 · [ACOG PB 205] |
